# SPT Mentoring Platform — User Guide

A guide for **Mentors** and **Scholars** using the Smallpeice Trust (SPT) Mentoring Platform.

> This guide covers everyday use of the platform. If you get stuck or spot something
> that doesn't look right, contact the SPT programme team — they can help with your
> account, your match, and any safeguarding concerns.

## 1. Getting Started

### Logging in

1. Go to the platform in your web browser.
2. Enter the **email address** and **password** SPT set up for your account.
3. Select **Log in**.

### Forgotten password

1. On the login screen, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email address — you'll receive a reset link by email.
3. Follow the link, choose a new password, and log in.

### Finding your way around

After logging in you'll see the main navigation bar. What you see depends on your role:

| Section | Scholars | Mentors | What it's for |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Home** | ✓ | ✓ | Your dashboard and an at-a-glance overview |
| **Messages** | ✓ | ✓ | Private one-to-one conversations |
| **Sessions** | ✓ | ✓ | Booking and managing mentoring sessions |
| **Goals** | ✓ | ✓ | Setting and tracking development goals |
| **Forums** | ✓ | ✓ | Group discussion topics |
| **Resources** | ✓ | ✓ | Shared documents and learning materials |
| **Surveys** | ✓ | ✓ | Feedback and programme questionnaires |
| **News** | ✓ | ✓ | Announcements and articles from SPT |

On a phone, the navigation collapses into a **menu** button (☰) in the top corner.

## 2. Your Profile

Open **Profile** (top-right of the screen) to keep your details current.

### Personal information (everyone)
- Name, photo, short bio, location, and contact details.
- Engineering discipline(s) and interests — these help with mentor matching and
  finding people with shared interests.
- A clear, friendly profile photo and a couple of lines of bio make a big difference
  when matching and starting conversations.

### Mentor profile (Mentors)
- Company, job title, and years of experience.
- **Specialisms** — the engineering areas you can mentor in. The SPT team uses these
  to allocate scholars to you, so keep them accurate.
- **Maximum scholars** — how many scholars you're able to support at once.
- General availability notes and your LinkedIn link.

### Scholar profile (Scholars)
- University, course, year of study, and expected graduation year.
- Your **goals for the programme** — what you hope to get out of mentoring.

### Shared documents
The **Shared Documents** area of your profile lets you securely share a file
(for example a CV) with a specific person by their email address.

### Notification preferences
Choose how you'd like to be contacted — see [Notifications](#11-notifications).

## 3. Your Mentor Match
*(Scholars)*

**You don't need to search for a mentor.** The SPT programme team reviews your
profile — your engineering discipline, interests, and goals — and **allocates a
suitable mentor to you**. This makes sure every scholar is paired thoughtfully and
that mentors are matched within the areas they can best support.

### What this means for you
- **Keep your profile up to date.** The more complete your engineering discipline,
  interests, and programme goals are, the better SPT can match you. See
  [Your Profile](#2-your-profile).
- **You'll be notified when you're matched.** Once your mentor is allocated, you'll be
  able to **message them** and **book sessions** with them directly.
- If you have a preference or your circumstances change, contact the SPT programme
  team — they manage all allocations.

> You can only exchange private messages and book sessions once you've been matched,
> so that conversations stay safe and relevant. Until then, you can still use Goals,
> Forums, Resources, Surveys, and News.

> **Mentors:** your scholars are allocated to you by the SPT team. When a new scholar
> is assigned, you'll be able to message them and arrange your first session — keeping
> your specialisms and availability current on your profile helps SPT match you well.

## 4. Sessions

The **Sessions** area is where mentoring meetings are arranged and recorded.

### How a session is booked
Once you've been matched, sessions are arranged between you and your allocated mentor.
1. A scholar **requests a session** from the **Sessions** area, choosing a time.
   Mentors can also offer **availability slots** that their scholars book into.
2. The session starts as **Pending confirmation**.
3. The mentor **confirms** (or declines) it.
4. After it happens, it's marked **Completed**.

**Session statuses:** Pending confirmation → Confirmed → Completed (or Cancelled).

### Joining the call
Confirmed sessions can include an online meeting link (Jitsi video call). The
**Join call** button appears when the session is taking place — open it at the
scheduled time to join your mentor or scholar.

### During and around a session
- **Agenda** — add the topics you'd like to cover so the time is well spent.
- **Notes** — both mentor and scholar can keep their own private notes.

### After a session — feedback
Once a session is complete you can leave **feedback**:
- A **rating** from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
- **Highlights** — what went well.
- **Improvements** — what could be better next time.
- Whether you'd recommend the mentor.

Feedback helps SPT understand how matches are going and supports the programme, so
it's worth completing after each session.

## 5. Goals & Milestones

The **Goals** area helps scholars set development goals and track progress, with
mentors supporting along the way.

### Creating a goal
1. Open **Goals** and create a new goal.
2. Give it a **title** and description.
3. Choose a **category**: Career, Technical Skills, Personal Development, Academic,
   Networking, or Other.
4. Optionally set a **due date**.

### Milestones and progress
Break a goal into **milestones** — smaller, tickable steps. As you complete
milestones, the goal's **progress percentage** updates automatically, giving a clear
picture of how things are going.

### Goal status
A goal can be **Active**, **Paused**, or **Completed**. Completing all milestones (or
marking the goal complete) records when it was achieved.

> **Tip for mentors:** reviewing your scholar's goals before a session is a great way
> to focus your time together and celebrate progress.

## 6. Messages

**Messages** provides private, one-to-one conversations between matched mentors and
scholars.

- Messages are delivered **in real time** — you'll see new ones arrive without
  refreshing.
- Open a conversation from the list on the left, type in the box, and send.
- You can only start a conversation with someone you're **matched** with.

Keep conversation on the platform where possible — it keeps a safe, supportive record
and means the SPT team can help if anything goes wrong.

## 7. Forums

The **Forums** are group discussion spaces open to scholars, mentors, and alumni.

- Browse forums and the **threads** within them.
- **Start a new thread** to ask a question or begin a discussion.
- **Post a reply** to join an existing conversation.

Forums are public to the community, so keep posts respectful and professional. If you
see something inappropriate, you can **report a concern** (see
[Staying Safe](#12-staying-safe--reporting-concerns)).

## 8. Resources

The **Resources** area holds documents and learning materials shared by SPT, organised
into **folders**. Browse the folders and open or download the materials you need —
useful for guides, templates, and reference material to support your mentoring.

## 9. Surveys

From time to time SPT will ask for your feedback through **Surveys**.

- Open **Surveys** to see any that are **available** to you.
- Complete and submit your responses.

Your answers help SPT improve the programme — please complete surveys when you can.

## 10. News

The **News** area is where SPT posts announcements, articles, and updates about the
programme. Select an article to read it in full. Check back regularly so you don't
miss anything important.

## 11. Notifications

Stay up to date with the **bell icon** (top-right) and the **Notifications** page,
where you'll find alerts about session requests, new messages, and other activity.

### Choosing how you're contacted
In your **Profile → Notifications** preferences you can control:
- **Email notifications** — on or off.
- **Browser push notifications** — if your browser supports them, you can opt in to
  receive alerts even when the platform isn't open. Your browser will ask permission
  the first time.

## 12. Staying Safe & Reporting Concerns

The safety of scholars is central to the programme.

### Safeguarding
- Mentors complete **DBS/PVG checks and safeguarding training** before being matched.
- Private messaging is limited to **matched** pairs.
- Conversations and shared content are kept on the platform so there's a safe record.

### Reporting a concern
If you see a message, forum post, or any content that worries you or feels
inappropriate:
1. Use the **Report a Concern** option on the content.
2. Add a short note describing the problem and **submit the report**.
3. The SPT moderation team is notified and will review it.

Some content is also screened automatically and may be **flagged** or **blocked** if
it breaches the community guidelines.

> **If a young person is at immediate risk of harm, contact the emergency services
> first, then notify the SPT safeguarding lead.** The reporting tool is for
> non-emergency concerns within the platform.

## 13. Quick Answers (FAQ)

**I can't see certain menu items.**
Menus depend on your role — some areas differ between scholars and mentors. If
something you expect is missing, contact the SPT team.

**I haven't been matched with a mentor yet — what do I do?**
Nothing — the SPT programme team allocates mentors. Make sure your profile (engineering
discipline, interests, and goals) is complete so they can match you well. You'll be
notified once your mentor is allocated.

**I can't message someone.**
Private messaging is only available between matched mentors and scholars. If you should
be matched but aren't, contact the SPT team.

**Where's the link for my video call?**
On the session in the **Sessions** area. The **Join call** button appears when the
session is due to take place.

**How do I change my email or push notification settings?**
Go to **Profile → Notifications**.

**I've forgotten my password.**
Use **Forgot password** on the login screen to reset it by email.

**I want to report something inappropriate.**
Use **Report a Concern** on the content, or contact the SPT team directly. In an
emergency, contact the emergency services first.
